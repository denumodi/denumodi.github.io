Why Emotional Granularity Outperforms Vague Mood Labels

Psychologist Lisa Feldman Barrett’s research on emotional granularity demonstrates that individuals who distinguish between closely related states—such as differentiating time-pressured frustration from sleep-deprived overwhelm or creative restlessness—exhibit 32% greater emotion regulation flexibility and recover significantly faster from workplace stressors.
