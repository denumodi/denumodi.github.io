How Biological Chronotypes Shape Daily Mood Trajectories

Positive affect (enthusiasm, mental energy, and optimism) follows an endogenous 24-hour rhythm controlled by the suprachiasmatic nucleus. Rather than remaining flat throughout the workday, human mood exhibits a bimodal pattern: a steep morning rise after sleep inertia dissipates, a mid-afternoon post-prandial trough roughly 7 to 8 hours after waking, and a late-afternoon secondary plateau where associative and creative thinking excel.
